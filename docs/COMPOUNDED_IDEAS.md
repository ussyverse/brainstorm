# Compounded Product Ideas: Final Ranking Across Twenty Passes

**Date:** 2026-09-13
**Status:** product synthesis and research handoff, not implemented integrations

## Coverage and evidence boundary

This synthesis read every line of `docs/ideas-01-developer.md` through
`docs/ideas-20-red-team.md`: all 20 brainstorming passes and 10,892 lines. It
extracted 188 serious proposals. Consolidating proposals that addressed the same
user, recurring task, shared entity, and end-user artifact produced 146
conceptual candidates. The ranking below selects 30 from that deduplicated set;
an omitted proposal is not counted as a second candidate merely because it had a
different metaphor or repository list.

The synthesis also uses `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and
`docs/SOURCE_CHECKED_COMBINATIONS.md`. Selected component source was inspected
under `/home/ubuntu/ussy/repos` on 2026-09-13. No candidate was installed, built,
or runtime-tested, no test result is claimed, and no end-to-end integration was
found.

Evidence labels have strict meanings throughout this document:

- **Exists:** the cited behavior is present in source that was inspected.
- **Proposed:** the adapter, shared model, interface, or user workflow must be built.
- **Unverified:** runtime behavior, domain validity, interoperability, or user demand was not established.

All health, safety, accessibility, legal, financial, food, historical, and
predictive outputs must preserve raw observations, assumptions, uncertainty,
and a human decision. A deterministic rule is not professional authority.

## Executive conclusions

1. **Keep the audiovisual planner first.** Twenty additional passes did not uncover another concept with a better combination of a natural shared object, visible before/after demo, bounded claims, and useful local exports. Build shot order and Foley first; color remains an optional third stage.
2. **The strongest commercial challenger is quote-to-capacity, not another CRM.** Lead identity, price evidence, and service capacity meet at one quote decision. `recapturaussy` + `bidussy` + `taktussy` is cleaner than adding `queueussy`, whose source contains elapsed-clock and fixed 40-hour-week assumptions that do not improve the first deliverable.
3. **Evidence packets are the portfolio's repeatable product shape.** Test calibration, CI diagnosis, agent regression, dependency review, archive release, and service improvement all become more credible when they preserve inputs, component findings, human decisions, and follow-up state instead of emitting a health score.
4. **Agent replay is substantive, but privacy is the gating feature.** `clanker03` and Hermes can support offline regression cases. Full blobs and prompts must not enter exported fixtures by default, and human labels must remain separate from observed behavior.
5. **Archive recovery is stronger than generic archive browsing.** Recovery manifests, content hashes, neutralized forms, claims, and operator context create a real release workflow. It cannot ship safely until an artifact-level export projection replaces Archivio's current all-artifact JSON export behavior.
6. **Accessibility products rank when they reduce decisions, not when they certify access.** Control/posture cards and known-route exit cards have focused artifacts and reversible interventions. They must not infer diagnoses, certify routes, or replace posted guidance and emergency responders.
7. **Several impressive metaphors fail at the authority boundary.** Portmore's customs-inspired license logic, Rosetta's zero-argument runtime witness, broad pipeline resonance, universal code-health scores, and automated preservation/disposal all overstate what their evidence can establish.
8. **Static source evidence is enough to choose contract tests, not a build winner.** The top three should be reranked after component builds, fixture round trips, and five target-user sessions each. A failed contract or weak recurring pain is a kill signal, not an invitation to add more repositories.

## Scoring method

Each dimension is a product judgment from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong). The total is
out of 25; it is a comparison aid, not measured market evidence.

| Dimension | A score of 5 means |
|---|---|
| Usefulness (U) | A named user repeatedly needs the output and can act on it. |
| Complementarity (C) | Components contribute distinct capabilities joined by one natural entity. |
| Feasibility (F) | A local vertical slice is plausible with limited adapters and no mandatory platform. |
| Evidence/maturity (E) | Core contracts exist in inspected source with bounded, honest claims. |
| Differentiation/demo (D) | The joined before/after artifact is easy to understand and hard to reproduce with one component. |

Ties favor a smaller irreversible-risk surface, a usable export before hosting,
and fewer semantic/unit conversions. Effort assumes one experienced developer,
a local single-user prototype, and successful component setup; it excludes
production hosting, synchronization, compliance review, and polished design.

## Detailed top 15

### 1. Shot-to-sound production packet

**User and output.** A solo maker or classroom film group arranges a short
sequence once and receives an editable missing-shot list, Foley recording/take
sheet, planning timeline, and optional color strip.

**Why it compounds.** Clip/scene order is a genuine shared entity. A moved
reveal can update editorial gaps, local sound-cue offsets, and supplied palette
beats. The product completes pre-production work rather than stacking analyses.

**Exists:** Kuleshov has [`ClipCard` and sequence analysis](https://github.com/ussyverse/kuleshovussy/blob/HEAD/src/engine.ts),
Foleya has [`SceneRequest`, `PlanScene`, cues, and CSV output](https://github.com/ussyverse/foleyaussy/blob/HEAD/foleya.go),
and Chromascript has [typed project analysis and artifact generation](https://github.com/ussyverse/chromascriptussy/blob/HEAD/src/lib.rs).
**Proposed:** a canonical project with stable scene/clip IDs, local fractional
offsets, an escaped editor, Go JSON wrapper, and mapping from color-beat indices
back to scenes.

**Evidence limit.** Kuleshov analyzes metadata, not pixels. Foleya neither
records audio nor provides frame-accurate sync and initially spaces beats by a
formula. Chromascript checks colors and intent supplied by the creator, not
audience response. Its current CSV is a planning list, not NLE interchange.

**Smallest proof.** Author a 60-90 second craft sequence, expose one missing
reaction/detail shot, add two Foley takes, move the reveal, and verify edited
sync points survive regeneration. Build shot order + Foley before color.

### 2. Quote-to-capacity desk

**User and output.** A repair, alteration, or field-service owner imports two
lead lists and recent quote outcomes, then receives reversible duplicate groups,
demand ranges, a reviewable quote queue, and visible capacity conflicts.

**Why it compounds.** Lead identity and service ID connect acquisition,
historical price response, and the work that can actually be delivered. That is
a tighter product boundary than a CRM or isolated pricing dashboard.

**Exists:** Recaptura implements [normalization, reversible alias merging,
overlap estimates, and warnings](https://github.com/ussyverse/recapturaussy/blob/2640919/src/lib.zig);
Bid implements [empirical/logistic demand estimates and expected revenue](https://github.com/ussyverse/bidussy/blob/4073119/src/bid/demand.py);
Takt models [services, capacity, demand history, and persistence](https://github.com/ussyverse/taktussy/blob/cf74d27/src/lib.rs).
**Proposed:** a stable lead/service schema, quote editor, unit/calendar policy,
follow-up state, and printable queue.

**Evidence limit.** Capture-recapture requires meaningfully independent lists;
it is not a count of real demand. Bid warns on small samples and is not a market
forecast. Takt's owner-entered capacity is a planning assumption. The owner must
approve merges and prices. `queueussy` is intentionally excluded: its elapsed
wall-clock rate and fixed-week assumptions add ambiguity before this workflow is
validated.

**Smallest proof.** Import two 20-row CSVs, reverse one bad alias merge, add ten
historical jobs, and produce one estimated-demand warning and one capacity
conflict without losing any source row.

### 3. Service problem improvement packet

**User and output.** A small service-operation owner links recurring station
problems to a complete metric series and a dated experiment/check record.

**Why it compounds.** The incident, metric, and experiment remain distinct but
join through one operational problem. This closes the loop that issue forms and
charts leave open.

**Exists:** Andonia has [event cards, recurrence grouping, and JSON reports](https://github.com/ussyverse/andoniaussy/blob/HEAD/internal/andonia/andonia.go);
Shewharta has [XmR limits, runs, trends, and intervention markers](https://github.com/ussyverse/shewhartaussy/blob/HEAD/pkg/shewharta/engine.go);
A3via has [observation-to-experiment workflow models](https://github.com/ussyverse/a3viaussy/blob/HEAD/internal/a3/model.go).
**Proposed:** persistent event capture, complete calendar buckets, stable
event/metric/case links, baseline selection, and one report.

**Evidence limit.** Missing periods cannot silently become zero incidents.
Shewharta's baseline is supplied, not proven stable; A3via's root-cause checks
validate form, not causality. A changed chart after an intervention does not
prove the intervention caused it.

**Smallest proof.** Load 20 daily counts for one packing defect, attach three
incident examples, record one checklist experiment, and show both an ordinary
series and a shifted series with raw counts and change notes intact.

### 4. Test evidence calibration packet

**User and output.** A maintainer receives a packet of repeated raw runs,
test-to-module gaps, uncertainty sources, repeatability findings, and the next
measurement to run instead of one test-health score.

**Why it compounds.** Acumen identifies structurally suspect evidence; Calibre
records how much confidence repeated measurements deserve. The handoff answers
"what should I measure next?" rather than "is the repository healthy?"

**Exists:** Acumen provides [test diagnostics](https://github.com/ussyverse/acumenussy/blob/403acf7/src/acumen/testigram.py)
and [flake analysis](https://github.com/ussyverse/acumenussy/blob/403acf7/src/acumen/flakegram.py);
Calibre provides [uncertainty budgets](https://github.com/ussyverse/calibreussy/blob/d9231bd/src/calibre/budget.py)
and [repeatability/reproducibility reports](https://github.com/ussyverse/calibreussy/blob/d9231bd/src/calibre/rr.py).
**Proposed:** shared run/test/module IDs, actual repeated-result ingestion,
evidence links, and report composition.

**Evidence limit.** Acumen labels its quality output heuristic and would need
real mutation data for stronger claims. Calibre depends on scientific
assumptions and numeric libraries. Neither establishes that a suite is good or
that a proposed change improved it.

**Smallest proof.** Import three runs of ten tests with one intentional flake
and one untested module; preserve every run and produce a specific repeat,
mutation, or manual-review action.

### 5. Trajectory-to-regression clinic

**User and output.** An agent maintainer converts one reviewed routing or tool
selection mistake into a redacted JSONL fixture and offline regression/practice
card.

**Why it compounds.** AgentReplay supplies the real incident, Hermes makes the
expected route repeatable, and Chainlet frames the procedural break and next
practice. The output is a fixture, not a transcript viewer.

**Exists:** Clanker03 stores [ordered sessions, events, blobs, snapshots, and
decisions](https://github.com/mojomast/clanker03/blob/e9f8608/agentreplay/schema.sql)
and implements [event, snapshot, and decision access](https://github.com/mojomast/clanker03/blob/e9f8608/agentreplay/store.ts);
Hermes has [deterministic golden-case routing evaluation](https://github.com/mojomast/hermes-agent/blob/cd941c4/agent/task_router_eval.py);
Chainlet has [break, dependence, fading, and guardrail analysis](https://github.com/ussyverse/chainletussy/blob/HEAD/src/chainletussy/analyzer.py).
**Proposed:** fixture minimization, secret redaction, stable task/tool IDs,
reviewer labels, and export policy.

**Evidence limit.** Recorded blobs may contain secrets. A golden route is a
maintainer expectation, not universal truth, and Chainlet's classifications are
heuristics. Rosetta is omitted from the MVP because constructing an inscription
can execute zero-argument callables; arbitrary runtime probing is unacceptable.

**Smallest proof.** Select one saved tool mistake, replace private values with a
fixture, produce one Hermes case, and rerun it without a live model. Unknown or
incomplete replay must never display as passing.

### 6. CI failure evidence packet

**User and output.** A maintainer sees the failed error beside exact environment
and configuration differences from a comparable successful run.

**Why it compounds.** Triage explains failure evidence while Petrichor supplies
the relevant historical configuration context. Their common entity is a run,
not a generic repository score.

**Exists:** Triage has [failure normalization and evidence extraction](https://github.com/ussyverse/triageussy/blob/HEAD/triage/extractor.py);
Petrichor has [configuration snapshot/history analysis](https://github.com/ussyverse/petrichorussy/blob/HEAD/src/petrichor/soil.py).
**Proposed:** run identity, captured configuration, normalized paths, baseline
selection, secret filtering, and the joined report.

**Evidence limit.** A nearby successful run is not automatically comparable.
Parsers and configuration coverage are ecosystem-specific, and config snapshots
can contain secrets. The report should say "difference to investigate," not
"root cause."

**Smallest proof.** Reproduce one fixture failure and one success with a single
meaningful environment difference, then verify irrelevant and secret fields do
not dominate the packet.

### 7. Recoverable mirror release capsule

**User and output.** An archivist recovering an authorized dead site gets a
capsule containing source captures, raw/final hashes, transformations,
unresolved links, operator context, explicit approval, and rollback pointer.

**Why it compounds.** Arrhivescrape explains how bytes were recovered,
Archivio records what is being claimed and its sensitivity, and Snapshot keeps
the selected run context. Repeated recovery becomes inspectable rather than a
pile of staging directories.

**Exists:** Arrhivescrape has [capture inventory](https://github.com/mojomast/arrhivescrape/blob/afffec1/archive_recovery/pipeline/inventory.py),
[normalization](https://github.com/mojomast/arrhivescrape/blob/afffec1/archive_recovery/pipeline/normalization.py),
and [validation](https://github.com/mojomast/arrhivescrape/blob/afffec1/archive_recovery/pipeline/validation.py);
Archivio has [artifact/claim storage and fixity](https://github.com/ussyverse/archivioussy/blob/918d690/src/archivio/storage.py);
Snapshot has [serializable run context](https://github.com/ussyverse/snapshotussy/blob/8351c8e/snapshot/models.py).
**Proposed:** authorized release projection, artifact-level filtering,
idempotent import, redacted context, approval, and rollback workflow.

**Evidence limit.** Recovery and matching hashes do not prove authenticity,
ownership, or publication rights. Arrhivescrape neutralizes recovered forms,
which is a useful default but not a complete security review. Archivio's current
JSON export filters claims but appears to include all artifacts; do not use it
for release until fixed and tested. Snapshot can retain secrets.

**Smallest proof.** Recover five pages from two dates, retain one unresolved
dependency, rerun, and show unchanged/new files. Export only an explicit
allowlist and prove a sensitive artifact cannot appear.

### 8. Replayable authored-content regression bench

**User and output.** A small game team turns a malformed reference, unreachable
room, or progression failure into a deterministic scenario with invariant,
replay command, state hash, and raw validator evidence.

**Why it compounds.** Cartographer checks spatial reachability, Feverfall checks
typed content/run rules, and Ludotape preserves deterministic actions. A content
edit can be tested against the same concrete failure.

**Exists:** Ludotape has [dispatch, replay, rewind, digest, and verification](https://github.com/mojomast/ludotape/blob/403d227/src/index.mjs)
plus [authoring helpers](https://github.com/mojomast/ludotape/blob/403d227/src/authoring.mjs);
Feverfall has a [typed replay runner](https://github.com/mojomast/feverfall/blob/e5f8084/tools/replay_runner/src/main.rs)
and [content linter](https://github.com/mojomast/feverfall/blob/e5f8084/tools/content_linter/src/main.rs);
Cartographer has [grid and directional audits](https://github.com/ussyverse/cartographerussy/blob/5e1cb8b/src/cartographer/audit.py).
**Proposed:** a stable scenario manifest, content/grid/action adapters, explicit
invariants, and raw-result attachment.

**Evidence limit.** Cross-engine semantic equivalence is unverified. Balance
simulation does not prove fun or fairness. Feverfall declares `UNLICENSED`, so
distribution or embedding is blocked until the owner supplies usable terms.

**Smallest proof.** Run one five-room grid and three encounters with an invalid
reference and unreachable room; require identical results/hashes twice and a
clearly changed case after the content ID is fixed.

### 9. Control and posture redesign cards

**User and output.** A person adapting a phone, appliance, desk control, or
craft station gets a text-first card separating target-acquisition changes from
sustained-use changes, followed by one reversible trial.

**Why it compounds.** The same observed task contains both control geometry and
the posture/exposure around repeated use. The user no longer has to reconcile
two unrelated checklists.

**Exists:** Fittsa accepts target geometry, neighbors, input mode, and posture
in [`TargetInput`/`analyze`](https://github.com/ussyverse/fittsaussy/blob/HEAD/src/lib.zig);
Rulaia accepts task observations, duration, repetitions, geometry, and
reversible interventions in [`TaskCard`/`analyze_card`](https://github.com/ussyverse/rulaiaussy/blob/HEAD/src/lib.rs).
**Proposed:** stable control/task IDs, accessible input, joined raw JSON,
before/after observation, and plain-text export.

**Evidence limit.** Both engines are coarse deterministic heuristics. They do
not certify compliance, diagnose a condition, or prove comfort. Do not infer
disability labels; preserve unknowns and the user's own terms.

**Smallest proof.** Record three controls, change one target or placement, and
capture before/after misses or hesitation. Every recommendation must point to a
supplied observation.

### 10. Low-decision outing exit card

**User and output.** A family, school, or friend group at a familiar venue gets
no more than three immediate exit actions, a primary/backup regroup point,
short leg clues, and a bailout.

**Why it compounds.** Egressa reduces group departure/regroup choices while
Controla reduces navigation ambiguity. The result remains consultable when the
group is tired or overloaded.

**Exists:** Egressa models [event observations, meeting points, buddy protocols,
and printable cards](https://github.com/ussyverse/egressaussy/blob/HEAD/src/egressa/engine.py);
Controla models [manual route legs, clues, controls, bailouts, time boxes, and
HTML reports](https://github.com/ussyverse/controlaussy/blob/c3170f9/src/controla.nim).
**Proposed:** a verified venue-feature editor, shared point/leg IDs, source notes,
and compact printable composition.

**Evidence limit.** Neither component has live maps, current closures, crowd
prediction, or a physical access audit. The product cannot certify safety or
accessibility and is not for emergency evacuation. Posted instructions, venue
staff, and responders remain authoritative.

**Smallest proof.** Enter one known event, three manually walked legs, two
regroup points, and an early-exit preference; print a card that remains useful
without a phone.

### 11. Confusion-to-contrast learning lab

**User and output.** A learner who repeatedly confuses neighboring concepts
gets a targeted contrast exercise linked to the original answer, confidence,
outcome, and later attempt.

**Why it compounds.** Cladwise identifies a specific conceptual collision while
Criterio makes the evidence and decision criteria explicit. A wrong confident
answer becomes a next learning action instead of another generic flashcard.

**Exists:** Cladwise provides [concept/contrast analysis](https://github.com/ussyverse/cladwiseussy/blob/HEAD/src/cladwise.ts);
Criterio provides [attempt, confidence, outcome, and report structures](https://github.com/ussyverse/criterioussy/blob/HEAD/src/criterioCore.ts).
**Proposed:** stable prompt/concept IDs, actual-answer capture, timestamps,
confidence vocabulary, generated contrast templates, and shared storage/UI.

**Evidence limit.** Concept extraction and confidence interpretation are
heuristic. The tool cannot infer mastery or a learning disability. Keep the
learner's answer and explanation visible beside any classification.

**Smallest proof.** Use ten attempts across two often-confused concepts, trigger
one contrast exercise, and show whether a later answer changes without erasing
the earlier evidence.

### 12. Household week pact

**User and output.** Roommates, partners, or a family get a dated weekly task
allocation with raw effort estimates, explicit disputed choices, linked votes,
and immutable completion history.

**Why it compounds.** The dashboard supplies recurring task instances,
Equilibria exposes allocation tradeoffs, and Quorum preserves only decisions
that require agreement. The vote can visibly change the plan.

**Exists:** FamilyDashboard has [task/profile types](https://github.com/mojomast/familydashboardussy/blob/HEAD/src/types.ts)
and [recurrence logic](https://github.com/mojomast/familydashboardussy/blob/HEAD/src/lib/recurrence.ts);
Equilibria has [allocation, fairness, envy, and ledger reports](https://github.com/ussyverse/equilibriaussy/blob/HEAD/src/lib.rs);
Quorum has [members, weighted ballots, vetoes, and tolerances](https://github.com/ussyverse/quorumussy/blob/397531d/internal/quorum/model.go).
**Proposed:** canonical task/instance IDs, explicit units/availability,
explanations, decision links, and printable export.

**Evidence limit.** Supplied effort and bids do not objectively measure labor.
Weights and vetoes must be visible, and proposed allocations must never apply
silently. Multi-user storage behavior remains unverified.

**Smallest proof.** Allocate four recurring chores between two people, reject
one assignment, record one decision, and ensure changing this week does not
rewrite a completed week.

### 13. Use-first kitchen loop

**User and output.** A household gets a board that prioritizes produce,
suggests ingredient-based uses and storage moves, then handles confirmed scraps
in a separate compost card.

**Why it compounds.** Condition, meal opportunity, and post-discard routing are
one item lifecycle, but the design keeps food decisions and compost decisions
separate at the safety boundary.

**Exists:** Ethyleno has [inventory, ripeness, storage conflict, and urgency
rules](https://github.com/ussyverse/ethylenoussy/blob/HEAD/ethyleno.c);
Mycelium has [dish, substitution, cluster, and leftover suggestions](https://github.com/ussyverse/myceliumussy/blob/efffc8e/src/mycelium/network.py);
Composta has [feedstock, moisture, phase, odor, and safety flags](https://github.com/ussyverse/compostaussy/blob/d27eaca/src/lib.rs).
**Proposed:** a typed kitchen-item lifecycle, dietary/serving inputs, explicit
discard confirmation, and board/export UI.

**Evidence limit.** Ripeness does not establish edibility; a flavor graph is not
a recipe validator; compost rules do not certify pathogen kill. Mold, allergy,
unknown condition, and local-rule cases need stop/refer-out states.

**Smallest proof.** Enter four produce/leftover items, show one storage conflict
and two use-first candidates, then manually mark one scrap before generating a
separate compost action.

### 14. Family archive with inspectable readings

**User and output.** A family historian links an uncertain transcription line
to a historical claim, source note, evidence link, counterclaim, confidence,
and verification task.

**Why it compounds.** Cartouche addresses what a difficult document might say;
Archivio addresses what someone is claiming from it. This preserves the gap
between transcription and interpretation.

**Exists:** Cartouche provides [line-oriented transcription and reading
structures](https://github.com/ussyverse/cartoucheussy/blob/HEAD/src/cartouche.ts);
Archivio provides [artifacts, claims, evidence links, counterclaims, sensitivity,
confidence, tasks, and fixity](https://github.com/ussyverse/archivioussy/blob/918d690/src/archivio/storage.py).
**Proposed:** stable line-to-evidence references, duplicate/source-independence
handling, review workflow, and safe export projection.

**Evidence limit.** A reading is not historical truth, a hash is not
authenticity, and multiple copied sources are not independent confirmation.
Archivio's artifact-level export privacy gap also applies here; use only test
fixtures until the projection is fixed.

**Smallest proof.** Transcribe five uncertain lines from two related documents,
attach one supported claim and one counterclaim, then export only a deliberately
public subset without local paths.

### 15. Local dependency change intake

**User and output.** A maintainer reviews one dependency update offline and gets
lockfile facts, public-interface differences, unusual project-pattern changes,
unknowns, and a human approval record.

**Why it compounds.** Package identity, changed interface, and deviation from
the local codebase are different evidence about the same proposed change. The
result is a dossier, not an automatic release gate.

**Exists:** Mint parses [npm lockfile entries and integrity/resolved fields](https://github.com/ussyverse/mintussy/blob/ade5430/mint/lockfile.py)
and represents [provenance chains and gaps](https://github.com/ussyverse/mintussy/blob/ade5430/mint/provenance.py);
Cambium has [AST/interface compatibility checks](https://github.com/ussyverse/cambiumussy/blob/5fe4d0d/cambium/compatibility.py);
Sentinel has [local Python pattern profiles and comparison](https://github.com/ussyverse/sentinelussy/blob/1b86f9c/sentinel/profile.py).
**Proposed:** package/release identity, baseline policy, ecosystem adapters,
raw-finding links, and an explicit approve/reject/defer record.

**Evidence limit.** Mint's provenance links accept caller-supplied signatures
and verification flags; they are not cryptographic verification. Cambium uses
language-specific ASTs and simplified version comparison. Sentinel detects
departures from local patterns, not maliciousness. Portmore is excluded: its
customs-inspired origin and governing-license heuristics are review prompts,
not dependable legal-obligation analysis.

**Smallest proof.** Review one Python or npm fixture with a changed signature,
one lockfile dependency, and one missing integrity/provenance field; preserve
raw source paths and require a human decision.

## Five compounded breakthroughs

These are design conclusions created by comparing proposals across passes, not
five additional products competing for rank.

### 1. The evidence packet is a reusable product primitive

Developer, business, archive, agent, learning, and household passes converged
on the same durable object: raw input + component findings + explicit unknowns +
human decision + follow-up observation. Implementing a small, domain-neutral
packet envelope could reduce plumbing, but its contents and decision rules must
remain domain-specific. Do not turn the envelope into a universal score.

### 2. Reversible normalization is often the real integration

The valuable join is frequently not another model. It is a stable identity and
an undoable mapping: lead aliases to source rows, clip offsets to scenes, CI
paths to runs, archive lines to claims, and agent events to redacted fixtures.
Every high-ranked adapter should store source IDs, transformation version, and
reversal information before adding automation.

### 3. A user-editable artifact is stronger than a recommendation

Suggested Foley times, quote estimates, route legs, chore allocations, and
learning contrasts gain value only when the user can edit, reject, annotate,
print, and later compare them. Regeneration must preserve accepted edits rather
than recompute the user's decision away.

### 4. Safety improves when stages do not silently feed each other

Several combinations are viable only with a hard boundary: ripeness cannot feed
an edibility verdict; byte similarity cannot feed deletion; archive confidence
cannot feed publication; route heuristics cannot feed accessibility
certification; license metaphors cannot feed release approval. The handoff
should expose two reports and require confirmation at the boundary.

### 5. Incidents can become regression assets across domains

An agent mistake, CI failure, broken game level, missed household handoff, or
failed service experiment can share a pattern: preserve the original case,
minimize it without changing the failure, state an expected invariant, replay
offline where possible, and record the result. This is more useful than a trend
dashboard when the recurring job is preventing the same failure.

## Rejected and demoted patterns

1. **Universal health/readiness scores.** Do not average Mint, Cambium, Portmore, Acumen, Calibre, pipeline resonance, churn, or test signals. Their units and evidence answer different questions.
2. **Authoritative license release gates.** Portmore can surface files and review prompts, but restrictiveness, "essential character," and customs-style origin heuristics do not establish legal obligations or a governing license.
3. **Automatic archive, photo, or household disposal.** Similarity, MUSTIE lanes, material extraction, or retention scores may prioritize review; none grants permission to delete or discard.
4. **Arbitrary runtime documentation probing.** Rosetta's witness is too narrow and can execute zero-argument callables. Runtime checks require explicit fixtures and process isolation; static drift can stand alone.
5. **Generic agent/orchestration platforms.** Stacking routers, agent forks, dashboards, and memory systems adds infrastructure without a new end-user deliverable. Prefer one incident-to-fixture path.
6. **Predictive safety and accessibility authority.** Manually authored route, heat, posture, repair, and hazard heuristics cannot certify a route, diagnose a person, predict an emergency, or replace current official information.
7. **Cross-domain metaphor pipelines.** Aquifer/Cavity/Telegrapha handoff rehearsal is intellectually coherent but semantically unvalidated. It should not predict incidents or capacity without measured domain mappings.
8. **LLM narration before evidence.** Generated root-cause, archive, service, or health narratives can hide missing evidence. Establish IDs, raw artifacts, redaction, correction, and abstention first.
9. **Privacy by export-profile name.** Archivio demonstrates why a "public" profile is not enough: artifact filtering must be enforced and tested at serialization boundaries.
10. **Shipping unlicensed dependencies.** Feverfall can support local contract research, but no distributable product should embed it while its workspace is `UNLICENSED`.
11. **Mega household/civic dashboards.** Combining chores, food, comfort, travel, assets, and safety creates data entry and authority problems without one recurring completed task.
12. **Automatic pricing, market, or capacity truth.** Small historical samples and user-entered work hours support scenarios, not market-rate recommendations or promises to customers.

## Balanced five-item portfolio

This is a portfolio of independent vertical slices, not one application. It
balances buyer types and technical risks while keeping shared infrastructure to
an evidence-packet envelope and local export conventions.

| Portfolio slot | Product | Why include it | First boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative flagship | #1 Shot-to-sound production packet | Clearest demo and lowest authority risk; tests multi-language orchestration with an artifact creators understand. | Shot order + Foley only; color after timing round trips. |
| Paid operations | #2 Quote-to-capacity desk | Direct pre-sale value for a small operator and a natural quote/service identity. | Manual CSV and capacity; no CRM, payments, or market pricing. |
| Developer trust | #4 Test evidence calibration packet | Entirely Ussyverse, local-first, and differentiated from another pass/fail dashboard. | Imported repeated runs; no universal quality score. |
| Preservation/public interest | #7 Recoverable mirror release capsule | Makes an existing substantive recovery pipeline safer and repeatable. | Fixture site and allowlist export; no public publish. |
| Accessibility utility | #9 Control and posture redesign cards | Small, text-first, reversible user outcome with a distinct audience. | User-authored observations; no diagnosis or compliance claim. |

Do not build all five at once. Run contract tests and customer discovery in
parallel, then fund one flagship and at most one lower-level packet/export
library. Avoid a shared database schema until two products prove the same fields
are genuinely stable.

## Validation plan for the top three

### #1 Shot-to-sound production packet

1. **Component contract gate:** install each checkout in isolation; run its documented tests/CLI; record exact toolchains and licenses. Feed one authored fixture through `analyzeSequence`, a small Foleya JSON wrapper, and Chromascript's CLI. Kill or rescope if user-authored data cannot round-trip without fixture-only code.
2. **Persistence gate:** define five scenes with fractional local offsets, edit two cue times, reorder a scene, regenerate, and assert stable IDs and human edits survive. Never use Kuleshov's lossy CSV as the editable source of truth.
3. **Task test:** give five solo creators the same 60-90 second brief. Measure whether they can create a useful capture/recording list in under 20 minutes and whether they actually consult it during one shoot.
4. **Comparison:** compare the joined packet with a plain spreadsheet template. Continue only if at least three of five users identify a missed shot/sound or complete a revision materially faster.
5. **Kill criteria:** stop adding color or automation if creators do not share shot order across the three jobs, if edited timing is routinely discarded, or if the wrapper takes longer than building a simpler checklist directly.

### #2 Quote-to-capacity desk

1. **Component contract gate:** build/run Recaptura, Bid, and Takt separately with a synthetic fixture; pin the revision and actual input/output schemas. Test empty data, two leads, fewer than ten quote outcomes, unknown service IDs, and zero/partial capacity.
2. **Identity gate:** import two source CSVs with obvious duplicates and one deceptive near-match. Require reversible merges, retained raw rows, and an audit record. Estimated unseen leads must be labeled separately from observed leads.
3. **Scenario gate:** vary quote price, owner-supplied hours, and one job duration. Ensure the packet shows a range and assumptions rather than an optimal price or promised delivery date.
4. **Task test:** interview and observe five owners who currently quote from inboxes/spreadsheets. Use their redacted historical records where permitted; ask whether the output changes one real quote or follow-up decision.
5. **Kill criteria:** stop if list overlap is not common, owners lack enough comparable outcomes, service units cannot be normalized without bespoke consulting, or the report creates more false confidence than the existing calendar/spreadsheet.

### #3 Service problem improvement packet

1. **Component contract gate:** run documented tests/CLIs for Andonia, Shewharta, and A3via; verify package boundaries, JSON persistence, date parsing, and exports. The Go `internal/` packages may require CLI wrappers rather than imports.
2. **Data-quality gate:** build a 20-day fixture with closures, missing observations, genuine zero-event days, changed workload, and one process annotation. Assert missing never becomes zero and counts are not described as rates without denominators.
3. **Linkage gate:** preserve event IDs -> recurrence group -> metric -> A3 case/experiment through edits and export. Show baseline selection and process-change notes beside the chart.
4. **Task test:** ask five small-service operators to log one recurring problem for two weeks. Measure completion burden, whether they select a concrete experiment, and whether the scheduled check actually happens.
5. **Kill criteria:** stop if event capture is too burdensome, comparable denominators cannot be obtained, owners cannot distinguish observation from explanation, or the packet becomes a generic ticket board plus chart.
